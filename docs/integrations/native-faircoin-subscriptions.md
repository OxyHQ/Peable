# Native FairCoin subscription periods

Peable now executes USD-priced, inclusive monthly subscription invoices through its existing watch-only FairCoin payment intents. Oxy is the financial owner for Oxy One and declared Alia product offers. No automatic debit or wallet signing authority is introduced. Each renewal requires the payer to open a new quoted checkout and authorize a transfer.

FairCoin is one rail beside the fiat providers, never instead of them (ADR 0010). `PEABLE_BILLING_COHORT` takes `{ "rails": [ { "rail": "stripe", …existing Stripe cohort… }, { "rail": "faircoin", … } ] }` in preference order; requests that name no rail keep reaching the first one, so existing Stripe subscribers are unchanged. A payer chooses FairCoin through `billing.listRails()` and `ensureCustomer({ …, rail: "faircoin" })`; the customer's rail carries its checkouts and subscriptions. The FairCoin entry (or, alone, the legacy single `rail: "faircoin"` shape) uses the strict `faircoinBillingConfigurationSchema`: explicit owner cohorts, reviewed offers, `priceSource: "faircoin_explorer_indexed_usd"`, `taxQuoteAdapterRef`, and `invoiceSigningKeyId`. FairCoin does not require a Stripe credential. Production requires a mainnet merchant watch-only xpub; nonproduction uses testnet. Configuration never enables a foreign application's merchant.

`PEABLE_BILLING_FISCAL_FILE` selects a reviewed declaration file parsed by `configuredFiscalAuthoritySchema`. `PEABLE_BILLING_INVOICE_PRIVATE_KEY_FILE` is the path of the operator-owned Ed25519 signing key, separate from wallet keys. In production it is the SSM SecureString `/oxy/peable/BILLING_INVOICE_PRIVATE_KEY`, mounted read-only by oxy-infra's `secret_files`. Its public half is pinned as `PEABLE_BILLING_AUTHORITY_KEYS["peable-billing-2026-10"]` in `@peable.to/shared-types` / `@peable.to/sdk`; the gateway refuses an unpinned key, and `invoiceSigningKeyId` and the fiscal declaration's `keyId` must both be that id. Consumers pin the same public key for invoice and tax authority. No private key belongs in a repository, browser, app or customer request.

Each fiscal declaration must specify its owner, legal seller name, actual assigned tax identifier, registration jurisdiction, exact product price and inclusive treatment, seller/issuer/remitter/service IDs, covered countries and coverage evidence, country rates with evidence and validity dates, and reviewed customer-location evidence references. Inclusive net is rounded half up in minor units. Even a zero rate must be explicitly reviewed and evidenced. Location is never inferred from IP, timezone, language or display currency.

Validate deployment files before boot:

```
DATABASE_URL=<local-test-database> bun packages/backend/scripts/validate-native-billing.ts cohort.json reviewed-fiscal.json
```

This validates shapes and exact price bindings, not legal correctness or network readiness. `configuredFiscalAuthority.test.ts` contains a machine-validated **synthetic** declaration and signature round-trip through the existing verifier. It is not a tax table for production. `config/oxy-fiscal-intent.json` records the user's worldwide commercial intention and user-attested Delaware C Corporation identity. The actual EIN and incorporation identifiers are held in private operator configuration, not in the public draft. These facts do not supply reviewed tax registrations, rates or customer-location rules; the draft deliberately is not executable fiscal configuration. Worldwide sales remain the objective; only countries with applicable reviewed declarations can produce a payment quote.

The official FairCoin Explorer `/api/price` returns `{ price, source: "wfair-base", updatedAt }`. Its USD reference is the indexed WFAIR/USDC pool on Base (GeckoTerminal, with Dexscreener fallback), used by the Explorer as the FAIR reference under the bridge’s intended 1:1 backing. Accepting this commercial reference does not verify bridge reserves or guarantee redemption; the configured `priceSource` must still be explicitly approved. The source timestamp must be fresh (at most two minutes old). There is no cached or invented fallback. USD cents convert exactly to FAIR base units with upward rounding. A quote expires after ten minutes or earlier when its fiscal evidence expires. The exact deadline is also persisted on the underlying intent, so direct intent URLs cannot extend it. Display-currency estimates never alter the commercial USD price or authorized FAIR transfer.

A payer-reported txid does not lock a rate. Only the settlement watcher observing the full amount at the reserved address establishes `acceptedAt`. Confirmations can arrive after quote expiry if that trusted observation was timely. A payment first observed late has no paid invoice authority and grants no entitlement; it requires operator reconciliation. A quote's signature is single-use across invoices, enforced by a PostgreSQL unique index. The same checkout/idempotency key replays the same address and invoice.

Renewal is available during the final seven days or after expiry, through `billing.renewSubscription`. Prepaying the next month extends the contiguous paid horizon without moving today's access start into the future. Cancellation forbids subsequent invoices, preserves historical paid periods and honors already issued quotes until their short deadline; it neither initiates nor signs a refund. No paid or refund status is fabricated. Expiry without payment ends access; no grace period is assumed.

A new initial checkout may replace a prior subscription only when every prior intent is terminal (`expired` or `rejected`), has no verified acceptance, and no paid history exists. The prior record is retained and retired under the same subject/plan lock. This is not proof that no funds were ever sent: the payer should check their wallet before explicitly authorizing a new quote. Missing materialization, pending broadcasts and confirmations keep their reservation. Failed chain observations do not distinguish zero matching output from partial funds; they require reconciliation, not another automatic charge. The SDK returns its existing `reconciliation_required` error for these failures, late acceptance, and unobserved broadcasts after quote expiry. Oxy preserves the pending reservation, checks the owning SDK before returning a saved URL, and exposes `PAYMENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED` to Accounts and Alia.

Oxy uses its persisted checkout authority and `retrieveCheckoutOperation` to recover a lost response, and reconciles immutable invoice IDs through the SDK. Public hosted checkout receives only the authorized USD/FAIR quote; private seller/customer-location evidence stays server-side. The shared display selector is presentation-only.

Verification includes real PostgreSQL migrations and contention, repeated/lost replies, owner boundaries, single-use tax quotes, actual settlement watcher processing with injected Explorer transaction fixtures, fabricated early txids, delayed confirmations and explicit renewal/cancellation. Read-only public Explorer access was demonstrated on 2026-10-15 with an explicit client User-Agent (mainnet height 195463; testnet height 0 and no connections). That exposed and corrected the old price response parser in billing and the display SDK. The source supplies USD only: USD/FAIR conversion works, and EUR returns unavailable without an authoritative cross rate. Run `bun packages/backend/scripts/probe-native-faircoin-readiness.ts` to check current public quote and chain availability without creating a checkout. External payment settlement has **not** been demonstrated; no funded test wallet is configured. No funds were spent, credentials published, or service deployed.

Individual offers may explicitly use `interval: "year"` with the existing annual USD total. The native subscription freezes cadence; renewal requires another explicit payment for the same cadence. One remains monthly at its central authority. Annual monthly quota windows are owned by Oxy, not Peable or Alia.
