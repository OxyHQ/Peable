# Going live

The same code runs live: what changes is the credential, and on the FairCoin
rail the network.

1. **A production credential.** Issue a service credential on your Oxy
   application in its `production` environment, with the same scopes
   (`payments:read`, `payments:write`). Its token selects live mode; nothing
   in your requests changes ([Testing](/testing#test-mode-and-live-mode)).
2. **A live merchant with its FairCoin key.** Your production credential
   resolves to its own merchant: register it as you did in test mode, with a
   **`mainnet`** watch-only key (or add the key once with
   `peable.merchants.faircoin.register`, or in Orbit). The key is required:
   every order offers FairCoin, so a live merchant without one is refused
   with `409 faircoin_account_required` when it creates a PaymentIntent, a
   Checkout Session, a Payment Link or a subscription, or finalizes an invoice
   ([FairCoin on every order](/faircoin#your-faircoin-receiving-key-is-required)).
3. **A live webhook endpoint.** Endpoints, events and secrets belong to a
   mode. Create the endpoint with the production credential, store its new
   `whsec_…`, and keep `https` — plain `http` is refused in live mode.
4. **A live provider account** for card payments, onboarded through Peable
   ([Connect and money](/connect#your-account)). Until onboarding finishes,
   card payments answer `409 account_not_ready`.

## Before you switch

- [ ] Every fulfilment runs from a verified webhook, never from a redirect.
- [ ] Every `POST` that moves money carries a key you can repeat after a crash
      (`Idempotency-Key`, or `external_ref` on refunds and transfers).
- [ ] Stock is released on `payment_intent.canceled`, never on your own clock,
      and never on `payment_intent.payment_failed` alone.
- [ ] Errors are handled by `type` and `code`, and the `request_id` is logged.
- [ ] Refunds and transfers read `status` and expect a later event to move it.
- [ ] The whole flow passed in test mode on testnet, card and FairCoin,
      including FairCoin chosen on a fiat order.

> **Live payments for third parties**
>
> Until Peable's legal release gate for third-party live payments is signed
> off, a deployment answers `403 live_payments_gated` to a non-Oxy merchant
> opening a live account, taking a live destination charge or instructing a
> live payout.
