# Peable

Peable is the payments platform of the Oxy ecosystem: one API, one SDK
(`@peable.to/sdk`) and a hosted checkout for accepting payments on two rails.

- **FairCoin, non-custodially.** The payer pays from their own wallet straight
  into the **Peable wallet of the Oxy account that owns your application**.
  Peable derives each address from that account's public identity key, watches
  the chain and never holds the coins — it cannot spend, refund or move them.
- **Cards**, through Peable's payment provider: card data is collected by the
  provider's own fields in the payer's browser and never reaches Peable or
  your server. Peable holds the provider credentials; you supply none.

Every order a payer completes on Peable (a Checkout Session, a Payment Link or a
hosted invoice) offers FairCoin beside the fiat methods you turn on, and
nothing turns it off. A fiat order paid in FairCoin is quoted in FAIR
([FairCoin on every order](/faircoin)).

Peable accepts FairCoin as a payment method. It never buys, sells or exchanges
FairCoin.
